Common questions

How do I import my Xcode colors?

Simply drag one or more .colorset folders into Resors. The app validates them instantly and displays their color values for all appearance variants — so you catch issues early, whether you're importing a single color or a full design system.

Alternatively, use the Import command-bar button to launch the file picker.

Is Resors free? What is Resors+?

Resors is free to download and use. Creating, importing, validating, organizing, and previewing colors, symbols, and images all come at no cost.

Resors+ is an optional subscription that unlocks the pro workflow — exporting Xcode-ready assets and two-way Xcode sync. You can subscribe any time from within the app.

How does syncing with Xcode work?

Two-way Xcode sync is part of Resors+. Connect your project, choose a catalog, and synchronize changes in both directions — edits made in Resors flow to Xcode, and changes in Xcode flow back to Resors.

Flexible conflict-resolution modes — letting you choose which version wins when both sides have changed — are coming in a future update.

What does “auto-detect” mean?

Resors automatically identifies color variants (Light, Dark, High Contrast) within your .colorset folders and previews them side by side with their exact color values.

Can I export colors back to Xcode?

Yes — exporting is a Resors+ feature. Resors generates Xcode-ready .colorset files in seconds, so you can export individual assets or entire libraries with guaranteed compatibility, or keep a connected project up to date automatically via sync.

Can I reuse colors across multiple apps?

Yes. Organize colors into reusable groups and libraries — define your palette once and use it across all your apps with consistency.

Does Resors support multiple colorsets at once?

Yes. You can import and manage multiple .colorset files simultaneously, making it easy to review and verify entire color libraries.

What color data does Resors show?

Each appearance variant includes HEX, RGB and Float RGB values, and each color value can define a color space — so you can precisely verify color consistency across Light, Dark, and High Contrast modes.

Why do my colors look different in Xcode?

Ensure your exported assets use the same color space as your Xcode project (sRGB is recommended). Resors previews colors using accurate color profiles to minimize discrepancies.
