Common questions

How do I import my Xcode colors?

Drag one or more .colorset folders into Resors. The app validates them on arrival and shows their color values for every appearance, so problems surface before the assets reach your project.

You can also use the Import button in the command bar to open a file picker.

Is Resors free? What is Resors+?

Resors is free to download and use. Creating, importing, validating, organizing, and previewing colors, symbols, and images all come at no cost.

Resors+ is an optional subscription that adds exporting and two-way Xcode sync. You can subscribe at any time from inside the app.

How does syncing with Xcode work?

Two-way Xcode sync is part of Resors+. Connect your project and choose a catalog. Edits made in Resors reach Xcode, and changes made in Xcode come back to Resors.

Conflict-resolution modes, which let you choose which version wins when both sides have changed, are coming in a future update.

What does “auto-detect” mean?

Resors automatically identifies color variants (Light, Dark, High Contrast) within your .colorset folders and previews them side by side with their exact color values.

Can I export colors back to Xcode?

Exporting is a Resors+ feature. Resors generates .colorset files Xcode reads directly, so you can export a single asset or an entire group. A connected project can also stay current through sync.

Can I reuse colors across multiple apps?

Yes. Organize colors into groups, then define a palette once and use it across your apps.

Does Resors support multiple colorsets at once?

Yes. You can import and manage multiple .colorset files at once, which makes reviewing a whole group practical.

What color data does Resors show?

Each appearance includes HEX, RGB, and Float RGB values, and each color value can define a color space. That lets you check consistency across Light, Dark, and High Contrast.

Why do my colors look different in Xcode?

Ensure your exported assets use the same color space as your Xcode project (sRGB is recommended). Resors previews colors using accurate color profiles to minimize discrepancies.
