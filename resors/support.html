Resors Support

Answers to common questions, and a direct line if you need one.

Common questions
How do I import my Xcode assets?

Drag one or more .colorset, .symbolset or .imageset folders into Resors, or drop a whole catalog. The app validates them on arrival and shows every appearance with its exact values, so problems surface before the assets reach your project.

You can also use the Import button in the command bar to open a file picker.

Is Resors free? What is Resors+?

Resors is free to download and use. Creating, importing, validating, grouping and previewing colours, symbols and images all come at no cost.

Resors+ is an optional subscription that adds export and two-way Xcode sync, including conflict resolution. You can subscribe at any time from inside the app.

How does syncing with Xcode work?

Two-way Xcode sync is part of Resors+. A group is paired with one asset catalog and stays paired with it. Sync runs when you launch it, never in the background, and a single pass covers everything: whatever landed in Xcode comes in, your edits go out, and anything that changed on both sides is settled by your rule.

Three conflict modes work today: keep Resors, keep Xcode, or keep the newest edit. A fourth, which holds both values so you can choose per asset, is expected in v1.4.

What does “auto-detect” mean?

Resors automatically identifies colour variants (Light, Dark, High Contrast) within your .colorset folders and previews them side by side with their exact values.

Can I export assets back to Xcode?

Export is a Resors+ feature. Resors writes .colorset, .symbolset and .imageset files Xcode reads directly, in one pass, for a single asset or an entire group. Every appearance, colour space and symbol margin comes with them. A paired group can also stay current through sync.

Can I reuse assets across multiple apps?

Yes. That is what groups are for: save a set of assets once and it becomes the starting point for the next project, instead of copied folders that slowly drift apart.

Can I work on many assets at once?

Yes. Import, preview, duplicate, export or delete a whole selection in one go, which makes reviewing a whole group practical.

What colour data does Resors show?

Each appearance includes HEX, RGB and Float RGB values, and each value carries an explicit colour space. That lets you check consistency across Light, Dark and High Contrast, and it is what gets written on export.

Why do my colours look different in Xcode?

Check that your exported assets use the same colour space as your Xcode project; sRGB is the usual choice. Resors previews with accurate colour profiles, and shows the colour space on every value, so a mismatch is visible before you export.
