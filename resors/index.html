01See itVisual

Your assets live in projects. Your design system lives in one grid.

Xcode previews your assets one catalog at a time, inside the project that owns it, at thumbnail size. Resors holds every colour, symbol and image you have in a single grid, across every project you ship, at a size you can actually judge.
