Umbraa

Made for the hours after dark.

Umbraa lives in your menu bar and takes your Mac's screen well below its built-in minimum brightness, so it stays comfortable in a dark room instead of glaring back at you. It can warm the colors too, or black the screen out completely when you're ready to sleep.

Color Comfort

Neutral, warm, or red.

Three ways to make the screen easier on your eyes. Neutral keeps the colors true, Warm shifts them amber for late evenings, and Red cuts nearly all the blue and green light. Try each one on the screen.
