What makes Umbraa different

Small, quiet, and exactly enough.

Dim below the limit

Soften every connected display past its darkest setting. Changes preview instantly, with smooth, gentle animation.

Color comfort

Choose Neutral, Warm, or Red. Warm and red tints ease dark-adapted eyes and keep the screen calm instead of harsh.

Sleep timer

Drifting off to a movie? Set a timer and Umbraa fades gently to black, so a bright screen never jolts you awake.

Automatic at sunset

Let Umbraa dim on its own from dusk to dawn. On at sunset, off at sunrise — all based on your location.
