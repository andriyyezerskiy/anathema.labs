Umbraa

Made for the hours after dark.

Umbraa makes your Mac comfortable to use in the dark. When even the lowest brightness is too bright, it softens every display with an elegant overlay that goes far beyond the system minimum.

  • Dim every display
  • Neutral, warm and red color comfort mode
  • Sleep timer with full screen blackout for late night viewing
  • Configurable automatic dimming from sunset to sunrise

What makes Umbraa different

Small, quiet, and exactly enough.

Dim every display

Adjustable dimming with gentle animation. Toggle it easily from the menu bar or with keyboard shortcuts — it works on all connected displays.

Color comfort

Neutral grayscale is perfect for any occasion. A warm glow is designed for evening use and better sleep, and a red tint reduces blue and green light.

Sleep timer

Set a sleep timer and your screen automatically fades to black with a full-screen blackout for late-night viewing. Easily dismiss it with a tap.

Automatic from sunset

Configurable automatic dimming from sunset to sunrise. Umbraa eases on at dusk and off at dawn, automatically.

Built for macOS

A native menu-bar app with external-monitor support, keyboard shortcuts, launch at login, and adjustable fade duration.

Almost invisible

Designed to feel like an Apple utility: calm, refined, and out of the way. No clutter, no learning curve.
