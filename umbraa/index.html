Umbraa

Made for the hours after dark.

Umbraa is a macOS menu bar app that dims your screen well below its normal minimum, so your Mac isn't painful to look at in a dark room. It can also warm the color and black the screen out when you're ready to sleep.

Screen Dimming

Go darker than your Mac allows.

Your Mac has a lowest brightness, and in a dark room it can still be too bright. Umbraa puts an overlay on top that takes every display much lower. Drag the slider to try it.

☀ ☾ 35%
☾ Tap to wake

Color Comfort

Neutral, warm, or red.

Neutral just dims the screen. Warm adds an amber tint that's easier on your eyes at night. Red takes out almost all the blue and green light. Pick one to see it on the screen.

☾ Tap to wake

Sleep Timer

Fades to black while you fall asleep.

Set a timer and the screen fades to black when it runs out. Handy if you fall asleep watching something and don't want the screen waking you up later. Start it to see the fade.

Sets a 45-minute timer
☾ Tap to wake

New · Sleep Now

Lights out, instantly.

One click blacks out every display right away, with no timer to set. Good for watching something in bed. Tap the screen to bring it back.

☾ Tap to wake
