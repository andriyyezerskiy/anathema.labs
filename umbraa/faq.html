Questions & answers

Umbraa, explained.

What is Umbraa? +
Umbraa is a macOS menu-bar app that dims every display below your Mac's minimum brightness, so your screen is comfortable to use in the dark — while watching a movie at night, reading in bed, or working late.
How is this different from just lowering my brightness? +
Even at its lowest setting, your Mac's backlight can still be too bright in a dark room. Umbraa adds a soft overlay that dims your displays far beyond the system minimum — and lets you warm the color, too.
Does it work with external monitors? +
Yes. Umbraa dims every connected display, including external monitors.
Can I warm the screen color? +
Yes — choose Neutral, Warm, or Red. Warm and red tints are easier on dark-adapted eyes late at night and keep the screen calm instead of harsh.
What is the sleep timer? +
Set a timer and Umbraa fades gently to black, so a bright screen never jolts you awake if you drift off to a movie.
Can Umbraa dim automatically at night? +
Yes. Enable sunset-to-sunrise automation and Umbraa eases the dimming on at dusk and off at dawn, automatically.
Does Umbraa collect any data? +
No. Umbraa does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.
Are there keyboard shortcuts? +
Yes — you can adjust dimming, toggle Umbraa, and start the sleep timer with keyboard shortcuts.
Does it launch at login? +
Optionally, yes. Umbraa can launch at login and start right where you left off.
